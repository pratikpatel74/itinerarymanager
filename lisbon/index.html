Getting Around

Lisbon's hills are best navigated by tram, metro and funicular. The Viva Viagem card covers all public transport. Tram 28 is iconic but crowded — walk the route at least once. Uber is cheap and reliable for the steeper neighbourhoods.

Neighbourhoods

Alfama – oldest district, Fado music, castles. Belém – UNESCO monuments and pastéis. Príncipe Real – boutiques and miradouros. LX Factory – weekend market and street art. Sintra – day trip, palaces and forests.

Must Eat

Pastel de nata at Pastéis de Belém (the original since 1837). Bacalhau (salt cod — said to have 365 recipes). Bifanas (pork sandwiches). Time Out Market Lisboa for a curated tasting of the best local chefs.
