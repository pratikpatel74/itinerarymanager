Getting Around

The Paris Métro (16 lines) runs until 1am and is the fastest way around the city. Buy a Navigo Easy card and top up with t+ tickets or a weekly pass. RER B links CDG airport directly to the centre. Vélib' bike-share is perfect for riverside rides along the Seine. The historic arrondissements are very walkable.

Neighbourhoods

Le Marais – Jewish quarter, galleries, Place des Vosges. Montmartre – artists' village, Sacré-Cœur, sweeping city views. Saint-Germain-des-Prés – Left Bank cafés, literary history. Champs-Élysées – grand boulevard, Arc de Triomphe, luxury shopping. Canal Saint-Martin – hip tree-lined canal, indie restaurants.
