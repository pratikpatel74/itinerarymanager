Getting Around

The T-Casual 10-trip card covers metro, bus and tram. Taxis and Cabify are reliable. The old city is very walkable — wear comfortable shoes for the Gothic Quarter cobblestones. Avoid peak-hour Las Ramblas pickpockets.

Neighbourhoods

Gothic Quarter – medieval history. Eixample – Gaudí buildings and upscale dining. El Born – boutiques and tapas bars. Barceloneta – beach and seafood. Gràcia – local, bohemian feel.
